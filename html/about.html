Our Story

More Than Events.
A Community of Artistry.

Choice Foundation was created with a simple belief — that beauty has the power to inspire, connect and create opportunities. We bring together talented makeup artists, beauty professionals and creative individuals through events, collaborations and community initiatives, helping them grow, get recognised and be part of a stronger ecosystem.
