More Than Events.
A Community of Artistry.

Choice Foundation brings together makeup artists, beauty professionals and creative talent through events, collaborations and opportunities. We believe in the power of artistry to inspire, create confidence and build a stronger, more connected beauty community across West Bengal and selected parts of Jharkhand.
